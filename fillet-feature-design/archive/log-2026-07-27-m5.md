# 2026-07-27 — M5: chamfer_tool / bevel_tool, the first real geometry

The operator stops being a no-op. `chamfer_tool` and `bevel_tool` now return a
solid; `fillet_tool` and `round_tool` still return nothing, because they need the
rolling ball subtracted and that is M7.

## What was built

Three new pieces in `fillet::detail`, on top of the M4 chain walk:

- **`chainNormals`** — the per-station averaged wall normals, lifted out of
  `spineFrames` unchanged. Both the tangency frame and the wedge section start
  from the same two normals, and only one of them existed before.
- **`wedgeSections`** — the pentagon `TA, TB, TB', v', TA'` at each station.
- **`buildWedgeSolid`** — hull each consecutive pair of sections into a cell,
  `BatchBoolean` the cells together. Closed chains wrap; junction cells are M8,
  so chains meeting at a branch vertex simply overlap there.

Convex is the concave construction with both signs flipped: setback into the
solid instead of across the reentrant quadrant, `eps` overshoot into the air
instead of into the material. One `dir = concave ? 1 : -1` rather than a second
code path.

## Two things worth writing down

**The setback is taken directly, and it matters.** `t = r·tan(φ/2)` would make
the argument mean an inscribed radius — 0.58t at 60°, 1.73t at 120° — which is
not what `chamfer(2)` means to anyone. The in-wall directions come out of
Gram-Schmidt on the two normals, and the two setbacks are independent, so
asymmetric chamfers cost nothing whenever we want them.

**Face-touching unions leave debris.** Consecutive cells share a section face
exactly, so the union's inputs meet in a set of zero measure. Manifold copes, but
per contact it emits a degenerate four-triangle shell: zero volume, no effect on
any later boolean, and enough to make `Genus()` report `-9` for a cylinder rim
that should be two rings. `Simplify()` does not touch them. `buildWedgeSolid`
decomposes the union and keeps only the components enclosing material — cheap,
and it makes the tool a solid anything downstream can trust, which is the whole
point of this milestone. Expect the same at M7 when `U` is built the same way.

`BatchBoolean` was otherwise a non-event, which is the interesting part: it had
no callers in the repo before this, and the determinism worry from the plan did
not materialize across repeated runs of the case suite.

## Verification

`fillet-tests/` is 48 checks, all matching expectations. The six that changed
state are exactly the chamfer and bevel ones — `tool`, `sandwich`, `emits` at
both sizes — and their expected-FAIL lines are deleted rather than updated. The
residual column of both pictures is empty, which is the same fact as the `tool`
check passing.

Five unit tests: the setback geometry, the wedge volume and bounding box on the
floor/wall union, the cube's twelve bevels, and the cylinder rim as the
closed-chain case.

The large variants confirm §6.5 is still open rather than quietly fine. A 40-cube
beveled at `t=30` collapses to the diamond formed by intersecting the twelve
half-spaces. That is the correct unclamped answer and a useless one; `has_ref` is
false for those variants precisely because clamping behaviour is not settled.
Nothing here decides it.
